How should I use AI at work if my company limits which tools I can use?

Answered by Shantanu Anand, AI Trainer & Consultant · 9 November 2026

Start by reading the written AI policy, or ask IT or compliance for it. Restrictions usually come from data protection, audit needs and the risk of wrong output in regulated work, not from distrust of you.

Use only approved tools for anything involving client, financial or personal data. For everything else, use placeholders and anonymised examples, and never paste confidential material into an unapproved tool. Keep a human check on every output that leaves your desk. If the rules block something useful, propose a low-risk use case to your manager, such as summarising public documents or drafting internal templates, and ask for a pilot.
