How do we keep AI-written code understandable to the team that has to maintain it?

Answered by Shantanu Anand, AI Trainer & Consultant · 10 November 2026

Treat AI as a fast drafter, not as the owner of the code. Every change should have a human who can explain it in a review without re-reading the prompt. If nobody can, the change is not ready to merge.

A few habits help. Keep AI changes small, so reviews stay meaningful. Ask the model to explain its design choices and write them into the pull request description. Require tests that a person has read and agrees with. Keep a short architecture note, written by humans, that the AI is told to follow. Finally, schedule regular time to refactor and delete code you do not understand. A blanket rule that everything must be AI-generated tends to cause the problem it was meant to avoid.
