FAQ
Frequently asked questions
Who is this programme for?

Founders, coaches, consultants, and small business owners who want to use Claude and related AI tools in their actual day-to-day work — content, research, proposals, and client communication — rather than a generic overview of what AI can do.

What happens in each session?

Each of the three sessions runs two hours, 1-on-1, and is built around your actual business. We work directly on your content, research, proposals, or workflows — not hypothetical examples — and each session pairs Claude with a complementary tool relevant to that stage of work.

Do I need any prior AI experience?

No. The programme is designed for people who are new to using AI tools seriously in their work, as well as those who've tried it before but haven't found it sticking to their actual workflow.

What do I need to have ready before we start?

Just a Claude account and whatever real work you'd like help with — a proposal you're drafting, research you need to run, or a workflow you want to set up. We'll set up anything else during the sessions themselves.

What happens after the three sessions end?

You'll leave with a working setup — prompt frameworks, a Claude workspace built around your business, and a plan for using it day to day — so the AI stays part of your workflow well after the programme wraps up.
