publishes it, and moves it to "## Published" with the date and link.
If "Up next" is empty, nothing is published that day.

  (optionally add notes on the next lines, indented, e.g. audience, key points)
- **Your own article:** paste it under a `### Article: <title>` heading.
  It will be lightly edited for clarity and SEO, keeping your voice and points.
