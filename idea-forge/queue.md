# Idea Forge queue

The daily Idea Forge task takes the FIRST item under "## Up next",
asks Claude to write the full idea entry, injects it at the top of
the #idea-forge section in index.html (newest first), and moves the
item here to "## Published" with the date and anchor id.

Add a new idea in either form:

- **Topic only:** `- Idea: Mobile pet grooming service in Indian cities`
  (optionally add notes on the next lines, indented)
- **Full idea:** paste the complete text under `### Idea: <title>` heading.
